## 2. Eligibility

**ABYB (Manipal)** [S4]. The page has two versions of some rules. Both are shown.

- **Degree:** graduates or postgraduates from any stream. "Graduation Degree (10+2+3 patterns) is compulsory." The page does not say whether 4-year degrees such as B.E. are accepted.
- **Marks:** "aggregate of 50% and above in all semesters of Graduation" (eligibility section). The FAQ says 50% in the final year **or** 50% aggregate across all years.
- **Final-year students** awaiting results can apply. Original mark sheets and degree must be shown on joining.
- **Age:** "not eligible if your age is more than 29 years 6 months" (eligibility section). The FAQ says "more than 30 years".
- **Credit score:** CIBIL score 700 or more. Students with no score (0 or -1) are eligible.
- **Arrears:** not eligible with uncleared arrears in graduation (FAQ).
- **Not eligible:** relatives working at Axis Bank; past disciplinary action or criminal case; pending legal action.
- **Universities not accepted:** Karnataka State Open University, Magadh University, Manav Bharti University, Monad University, Sunrise University, William Carey University, "Or any other universities that are not part of Axis Bank's hiring policies".
- **Experience:** freshers and people with work experience can apply.
- **Background check:** education is verified by an external agency before selection or employment.

**ABYB (Career247)** [S6]: 50% in graduation; no minimum for Class 10 and 12; age 21–30 at the time of application; 10+2+3 pattern compulsory; experience not required. Admissions open for Punjab, Haryana, Jammu, Himachal and Uttarakhand.

**ABYB (NIIT University)** [S5]: "Graduates | Age: Below 30 Years".

**Retail Lending Program** [S7]: any graduate; 50% or more in Class 10, Class 12 and graduation; full-time graduates only; CIBIL above 700 (or no score).

**Banking Operations Program** [S8]: any graduate; 55% in graduation; full-time only; age 28 or below; women only; CIBIL 700+ (or no score). Same list of universities not accepted.

**Axis Sales Academy** [S3]:

- Graduate in any discipline from a recognised college in India, at least 50%.
- Age 21–28.
- Fresher, or up to 9 months of experience.
- Valid driving licence and a two-wheeler.
- PAN card and address proof.
- Not a current or former Axis Bank employee; no close family at Axis Bank or its subsidiaries; no pending court case.

**ARISE (2023 cycle)** [S9]: any graduate or postgraduate; fresher or up to 60 months of experience; over 18 as on 31 Mar 2023; Class 10 and 12 (or diploma) from a recognised board; not enrolled in another Axis programme (ABYB, PBRM and others); not a current or ex-employee.

## 3. Selection steps

**ABYB (Manipal)** [S4]:

1. Register online and pay the ₹100 application fee. The fee "does not guarantee selection".
2. Level 1: proctored online written test.
3. Level 2: "Online AI Video Assessment".
4. Selected students get an admission (selection) letter.
5. Pay the programme fee and report to the Bengaluru campus.

- If you miss the online test dates, you get "only one additional attempt in a given cycle".
- During classroom training, passing NISM certification (Depository and Mutual Fund) is compulsory. Only those who pass may start the internship.
- You must clear every exam and stage with minimum marks to move on and become a confirmed employee.

**ABYB (NIIT University)** [S5]: application → "Career Readiness Assessment (Online)", described as "Online Assessment & NIIT Interaction" → enrol and pay the fee.

**ABYB (Career247)** [S6]: register and pay ₹118 → online assessments (same five sections as Manipal) → "Adda247 Skills Interview", a virtual interview with a faculty panel → selection and admission letter → enrol and pay.

**Retail Lending Program** [S7]: pay ₹100 + GST → Level 1 online assessment (English, Maths and Analytical Skills) → Level 2 personal interview with an Axis Bank panel → Axis Bank provisional offer letter → 4 months of training in Bengaluru.

**Banking Operations Program** [S8]: pay ₹250 → Level 1 written test → Level 2 personal interview → provisional offer letter → 3 months of training in Bengaluru.

**Axis Sales Academy** [S3]:

1. Round 1: Pre-Assessment, "An evaluation of quantitative aptitude and general knowledge".
2. Round 2: Virtual Training, six days, for shortlisted candidates.
3. Round 3: Post-Assessment after the training.

- Join within 7 days of the offer letter. If not selected, you can reapply after 6 months.

**ARISE (2023 cycle)** [S9]: pay ₹499 → Cognitive Assessment → Functional Assessment with a personality profiler → Elevator Pitch (video answers judged by senior leaders). All rounds were virtual. You could apply once a year.

**Training, bond and posting:**

- ABYB: 4 months of classroom training at Manipal, Bengaluru; 3 months of internship at any Axis branch in India; 5 months of on-the-job training, which is the final posting [S4].
- On completion: Post Graduate Diploma in Banking Services from Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) [S4].
- **Bond:** Not published. But: if you leave the ABYB programme, "The final Job offer stands null and void" and the fee is not refunded "under any circumstances" [S4].

- Sales Academy: branch within 10 km of your home location [S3].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Fee warning first.** ABYB, Retail Lending and Operations ask the student to pay a course fee of ₹2,15,000 to ₹2,54,228 before GST to the training partner. Axis Sales Academy has no fee. Lyra should show the fee next to the pay for every route, so that B.Com/BA/B.Sc students can compare.
- **Practise the ABYB Level 1 mix:** verbal, analytical (reasoning), numerical, a written English task and a listening comprehension task. Listening and writing are unusual. Lyra can add short audio passages with questions, and a timed email or paragraph writing task. The exact pattern and timing are not published.
- **Practise for an AI video interview.** ABYB Level 2 has no human panel. Students should practise speaking to a camera: self-introduction, "why banking", "why sales", and a short product pitch.
- **For Sales Academy,** practise basic quantitative aptitude and general knowledge (banking and current affairs). The question count is not published.
- **Eligibility check:** Lyra can warn students before they apply if they are under 50% in graduation, over the age limit, have uncleared arrears, have a CIBIL score below 700, study at one of the six excluded universities, or (for Sales Academy) have no two-wheeler and licence.

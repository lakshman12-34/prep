## 1. Tracks and pay

- **Graduate Trainees:** LTTS trains freshers in two programmes, **STEP IN** and **GENESIS**, run by its Global Engineering Academy (GEA). LTTS describes them as "Making Freshers Project-ready" [S1].
- **Role names, tracks and pay:** Not published. We found no LTTS job page and no college notice (2025-26 or 2026-27) that states the fresher role, CTC, stipend or bond.
- **TECHgium (student challenge):** LTTS's "flagship" open innovation challenge for engineering students [S1]. The 2025 (9th) edition offered cash awards of ₹18 L in total: winner ₹10 L, runner-up ₹5 L, second runner-up ₹3 L, and "potential recruitment opportunities with LTTS" [S5]. It is a competition, not a hiring track; the number of jobs offered through it is Not published.

## 2. Eligibility

- **Fresher hiring rules** (degrees, branches, marks, backlogs, gap, age, batches): **Not published.** No LTTS page or college notice found for 2025-26 or 2026-27.
- **TECHgium 10th edition (2026)** [S4]:
  - Open to 2nd-year, pre-final-year and final-year UG engineering students, and PG engineering students.
  - Any engineering discipline.
  - Individual or team entry, as the TECHgium rules allow.
  - The college's TPO, Dean or Principal must first register the institute. This creates a Unique Institute Code (UIC), which students need to register.
- **TECHgium 9th edition (2025):** final-year and pre-final-year engineering students (Bachelor's and Master's) [S5].

## 3. Selection steps

**Official steps:** Not published.

**What candidates report** (1 candidate account, published Oct 2023, updated Oct 2024; batch not stated) [S7]:

1. Online test.
2. Technical interview, in person.
3. HR interview.

- The account says 57 students from its college were selected for interview after the online test [S7].
- No 2025-26 or 2026-27 candidate account for LTTS fresher hiring was found on GeeksforGeeks (3 Oct 2026).

## 4. Online test

**Official facts:** Not published (sections, questions, minutes, coding, languages, negative marking, platform, cut-offs).

**What candidates report** (1 account, Oct 2023) [S7]:

- 90 questions: 30 logical, 30 quantitative, 30 domain.
- 2 coding questions.
- Time limit, platform and negative marking: not reported.
- This account is old. Use it only as a rough guide.

## 5. Interview topics

Candidate accounts only: 1 account, published Oct 2023 (updated Oct 2024) [S7].

- **Technical:** questions from the resume; OOP; SQL queries; basic C++.
- **HR:** "Where do you see yourself after 5 years?"; location preference.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **Fresher drive dates:** Not published for 2025-26 or 2026-27.
- **When freshers joined:** LTTS says it "onboarded freshers" in Q4 FY26 (Jan–Mar 2026) [S2]. In Feb 2025, CEO Amit Chadha said hiring of 2,000 freshers in calendar 2025 would be "in a phased manner" [S3].
- **How to apply:** through your college's placement cell. LTTS's careers pages (read 3 Oct 2026) have no fresher or campus section [S6].
- **TECHgium 10th edition (2026-27 season):** registration was open on 29 Sep 2026. Registration portal: https://ltts.techgium.com. Contact: TECHgium@ltts.com [S4].
- **TECHgium 9th edition (2025-26 season):** went live on 5 Sep 2025 [S5].
- **TECHgium size:** in its FY26 annual report, LTTS says 62,622 engineering students from 540 institutions registered "this year", and 34 finalist teams present at the Grand Finale at LTTS Mysuru [S1].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Say plainly that LTTS does not publish its fresher test pattern, pay or eligibility.** Lyra should ask students to read their own college's LTTS notice and should not show the job-site figures (₹4,00,100, 6.75 CGPA, etc.) as facts.
- **Practise general aptitude plus your branch's core subject.** The only candidate account (2023) describes logical, quantitative and domain sections of equal size plus 2 coding questions. LTTS is an engineering (ER&D) firm, so we advise students to also revise the core subjects of their own branch. This is our advice, not a published fact.
- **Interview:** prepare projects, OOP, SQL and one programming language (the 2023 account names C++), and an honest answer on location preference.
- **TECHgium is the one LTTS route with published rules and dates.** It is open from 2nd year onward, needs the college to register first (UIC), and the 2025 edition offered ₹18 L in prizes and "potential recruitment opportunities". Lyra can remind TPOs to register the institute when a new edition opens (the 10th edition was open on 29 Sep 2026).

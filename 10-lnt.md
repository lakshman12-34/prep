## 2. Eligibility

### DET 2026 (official form, archived 21 Jan 2026) [S2]

- **Diploma:** 3-year (6-semester) full-time diploma with at least 60% aggregate.
- **Graduates excluded:** anyone with a graduation in Engineering, Science or Arts (before or after the diploma) is not eligible.
- **Completion:** diploma finished by 30 Jun 2026.
- **Course type:** sandwich, part-time and correspondence courses are not accepted.
- **Backlogs:** no past or live backlogs. All papers must be cleared in the first attempt.
- **Date of birth:** 1 Jul 2004 to 30 Jun 2008, both days included.
- **Nationality:** Indian nationals only.
- **Medical:** final selection depends on medical fitness, as per the guidelines of L&T's Chief Medical Officer.
- **Streams (16):** Architecture, Automobile, Chemical, Civil, Computer Science & IT, Electrical & Electronics, Electronics & Communication, Electronics & Instrumentation, Environmental, EHS / Industrial Safety, Fire Safety, Instrumentation & Control, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Mining, Survey.
- **Year of passing (form drop-down):** 2024, 2025 or 2026.
- **Class 10 / 12:** no separate cut-off. The form accepts any Class 10 percentage from 35 upwards.
- **Gender:** the form offers Male, Female and Others. No restriction is stated.

### DET 2025 (official form, archived 20 Jan 2025; older) [S4]

- Same rules as 2026, except:
  - Date of birth: 1 Jul 2003 to 30 Jun 2007.
  - Diploma finished by 30 Jun 2025.

### JET 2026, L&T Heavy Engineering (live official form, 3 Oct 2026) [S6]

- **Date of birth:** the form accepts 1 Jul 2002 to 30 Jun 2007.
- **Marks:**
  - Class 10: at least 60%.
  - Class 12 (if taken): at least 50%.
  - Diploma (up to 6th semester / 3rd year): at least 60%.
- **Diploma end date:** no later than 30 Jun 2026.
- **Disciplines in the form:** Automobile, Electrical, Mechanical, Mechatronics. The poster says Mechanical / Electrical [S7].
- **Other questions in the form:** number of backlogs and mode of study.
- **Earlier L&T process:** you must not have taken part in any L&T campus process before [S7].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Test practice:** practise branch (core) MCQs plus aptitude: quant, English and logical reasoning. Officially the test covers domain knowledge and cognitive aptitude; the question counts and time are not published. The only breakdown is 1 candidate account from 2022, and Lyra should label it that way.
- **Core subject matters.** In that one account, core technical was the largest section (20–25 questions). Lyra's branch-wise question banks fit this route.
- **Interview practice:** branch basics, the final-year project, why you want to join L&T, and questions on relocation and shift work.
- **Show the strict rules on the card:** 60% aggregate, all papers cleared in the first attempt, no backlogs, the date-of-birth window, no graduates, and the medical. An eligibility checker would stop students who cannot apply.
- **Deadlines:** JET 2026 Heavy Engineering closes 5 Oct 2026. For DET, colleges are invited Aug–Nov, so Lyra can remind TPOs before then.

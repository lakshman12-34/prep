## The name change

- **11 Feb 2026:** the Board approved changing the company's name from LTIMindtree Limited to LTM Limited. A brand press release came out the same day [S2].
- **17 Mar 2026:** the new name took legal effect [S1].
- **Trading symbol:** changed from LTIM to LTM. The stock-exchange announcement was filed on 27 Feb 2026 [S3].
- **How the company writes it:** its letterhead reads "LTM Limited (formerly LTIMindtree Limited)" [S1].
- **Careers site:** ltimindtree.com/careers now redirects to ltm.com/careers. That page has no campus or fresher section. It links to careers.ltm.com/search/ and careers.ltimindtree.com/search/ [S4].
- **College notices use both names:** the 2026-batch notices (Sep 2025) say LTIMindtree [S12] [S13]. The 2027-batch notice (Sep 2026) says LTM [S11].

## 5. Interview topics

Candidate accounts only: 3 accounts from the 2025-26 season. Sep 2025 [S19] and Jan 2026 [S18] show question lists only. Jan 2026 [S17] is a full account. All 3 agree on project, SQL and HR basics.

**Technical round** (Jan 2026 account; round on 22 Jan 2026, 30–40 min, online) [S17]:
- A MERN-stack project (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js): which React concepts you used, how many APIs, MongoDB, how you would replace Node.js with Spring Boot.
- The four pillars of OOP; your internship.
- A scenario: a client asks you to deliver before a deadline that you know cannot be met.

**HR round** (same account; 23 Jan 2026, 15–20 min) [S17]:
- Are you really interested in this role? Why should I hire you? Areas to improve. Relocation.

**Question list, Jan 2026** [S18]:
- Remove duplicates from an array; time complexity of linear vs binary search.
- An SQL query that returns results in reverse order.
- Why LTIMindtree; strengths and weaknesses; your views on AI.

**Question list, Sep 2025** [S19]:
- An SQL query to find the third-highest salary.
- Are you willing to relocate?

**Competition at one campus:** one account summary (Jan 2026) says 24 were selected out of 600 applicants [S18]. This is a single account and one campus, not a company figure.

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Eligibility checks:** for the 2027 batch, use 65% in SSC, Inter and UG and no active backlogs (one college notice). For the 2026 batch the rule was 60% throughout. LTM publishes neither, so Lyra should name the college notice as the source.
- **Online test practice:** cover logical, quantitative and analytical ability; programming; computer-science basics; English comprehension; and spoken English. These are the sections in the 2026-batch college notice. The exact pattern (question counts and section times) is not published.
- **Spoken English:** it is a named section of the 2026-batch KIIT test, and the 2027 notice lists "communication". Students should practise speaking, not only reading.
- **Home-proctored test:** the 2026-batch test was taken from home with a webcam, a lockdown browser, formal dress and a plain background. Lyra can help students check this set-up before the test day.
- **Interview:** students should be ready to explain their own project in depth, the four pillars of OOP, basic SQL (third-highest salary, ordering results), simple array problems, and HR questions (why this company, relocation, strengths and weaknesses). Show the company as "LTM (formerly LTIMindtree)", as its own letterhead does.

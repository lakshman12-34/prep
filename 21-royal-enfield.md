## 6. Season and how to apply

- **Apprentices:** apply on **apprenticeshipindia.gov.in**. Royal Enfield's listings were posted on 8 May 2025 and 24 Sep 2025. On 3 Oct 2026 the portal showed 53 of 200 and 84 of 400 places still available. Closing dates: 31 Dec 2026 (Sep 2025 listing) and 31 Mar 2028 (May 2025 listing) [S2].
- **Graduates:** campus hiring "from various institutions across the country" [S1]. Season dates: **Not published.**
- **Careers portal:** careers.royalenfield.com had 84 open jobs on 3 Oct 2026. Searches for trainee, apprentice, intern, fresher, campus, ITI and NAPS returned **0** jobs [S8].
- **Royal Enfield Academy for Technical Skills** took part in Tamil Nadu government job fairs at Tenkasi (2 Aug 2025) and Coimbatore (22 Aug 2026), posting diploma and NTC vacancies with location Chennai [S4] [S5]. It is registered on the state portal as a trust/society at Oragadam–Walajabad Road, Vanchuvanchery, Padappai [S6].
- **Older college notices (2018–2020, old):** S.K.D.A.V. Government Polytechnic, Rourkela, posted Royal Enfield pool campus drives and an "urgent requirement" for diploma pass-outs of the 2018, 2019 and 2020 batches [S9]. No 2025–27 college notice was found.
- **Where you work:** Royal Enfield's four plants in Tamil Nadu are at **Thiruvottiyur, Oragadam, Vallam Vadagal and Cheyyar**, with the head office in Chennai [S1]. A ₹958 crore expansion at Cheyyar is planned to finish by FY 2027-28 [S1].
- **Fraud warning:** no recruitment-fraud notice was found on Royal Enfield's sites (checked 3 Oct 2026).

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **For diploma and 10th-pass students, the clearest live route is the NAPS apprenticeship** on apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (listing open until 31 Dec 2026). Lyra can show the stipend range exactly as listed (₹6,000–20,250) and the four Chennai-area locations.
- **Do not present 12,167 as "jobs".** It is the number of trainees trained in a year. Lyra should say "trained", not "hired".
- **Test practice:** Royal Enfield publishes no test pattern. Lyra should say "pattern not published" and offer general aptitude and basic mechanical practice only.
- **B.E. students:** campus hiring is small (45 graduates in FY26) and the engineering trainee scheme now targets people with some experience. Lyra should set expectations and not promise a GET route.
- **The Royal Enfield Academy** is free training, not a Royal Enfield job. Lyra can list it as a skill option for fresh diploma holders in Tamil Nadu, with a note that the report is old (2020-21 data).

## 1. Tracks and pay

- **Graduate Engineering Training (GET):** Coforge calls it a "Comprehensive boot camp model supporting campus talent" [S1].
- Coforge also runs "structured programs for campus hires and lateral talent" to speed up how fast new joiners become productive [S1].
- CEO Sudhir Singh (Apr 2026): "We have not changed our model of recruiting from colleges and campuses that some of our peers have stopped." [S2]
- **Role name, CTC, stipend, bond:** Not published. We found no Coforge page and no college notice (2025-26 or 2026-27) that states the fresher pay.

## 2. Eligibility

- **Branches (CBIT Hyderabad, 2027 batch)** [S3] [S4]: BE – CSE, IT, AI&ML, CSE(AI&ML), AI&DS, CSE(IoT, CS & BCT) and ECE. This is the list for one college; other colleges may differ.
- **Batch:** the CBIT drive was for the **2027 graduating batch**, run in Apr–May 2026, while students were in pre-final year [S3] [S4].
- **Marks, backlogs, gap, age, degrees outside BE:** Not published.

## 3. Selection steps

**Steps at CBIT Hyderabad, 2027 batch** (college notices that set out the Coforge process) [S3] [S4]:

1. **Online Aptitude Test** (90 minutes) and **online Spoken Communication test, "SpeechX"** (30 minutes), both on 15 Apr 2026, virtual, in a test window from 4:45 PM to 9:00 PM. Students must attempt both tests.
2. **Pre-placement talk (PPT)** on campus, 11 May 2026, 9:00 AM.
3. **Interviews** on campus, 11 May 2026, from 10:30 AM. The notice does not say how many interview rounds.

- Numbers at CBIT: 391 students registered for the online test; 107 were shortlisted for the PPT and interviews [S3] [S4].
- **Earlier season at CBIT (2024):** circulars dated 31 May 2024 ("Aptitude Test & Spoken Communication (SpeechX)") and 8 Jun 2024 ("PPT, Technical and HR Interviews") [S5]. We read only their titles.

**What candidates report** (1 candidate account, Jul 2024; on campus, "Software Developer" role) [S7]:

- Communication round first (Listening, Speaking, Grammar MCQs) → online assessment → one interview.
- No 2025-26 or 2026-27 Coforge candidate account was found on GeeksforGeeks (3 Oct 2026).

## 4. Online test

**Facts from the CBIT notice, 2027 batch** [S3]:

| Test | Minutes | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Aptitude Test | 90 | Online (virtual), within the test window |
| Spoken Communication (SpeechX) | 30 | Online (virtual), within the test window |

- **Negative marking:** "There is no negative marking in any of the section / test."
- **Cut-off:** "There is a sectional cut-off in the online test." The cut-off value: Not published.
- **What you need:** a laptop or desktop with a working webcam, and earphones with a mic for the spoken test.
- **Platform:** the sample audio link for the spoken English test is on Mettl (tests.mettl.com).
- **Not published:** number of questions, section names, coding questions, coding languages.

**What candidates report** (1 account, Jul 2024) [S7]:

- Online assessment had three parts: **Aptitude** (reasoning, data interpretation, quant), **Technical MCQs** (programming basics, OOP, operating systems, computer networks, SQL) and **Coding**.
- **Coding:** 1 question (fractional knapsack) in 25 minutes.
- Each part had a cut-off.
- This account is from the 2024 season. Use it only as a rough guide.

## 5. Interview topics

Candidate accounts only: 1 account, Jul 2024 [S7].

- Self-introduction and projects on the CV.
- **Databases/SQL:** DDL vs DML; write a query for students who scored more than 40%; normalisation.
- **OOP:** basic principles; run-time vs compile-time polymorphism; types of inheritance; abstraction vs interface.
- **Data structures:** arrays; linked lists and their types; making a circular list from a singly linked list; sort an array.
- **Coding on the spot:** Fibonacci series.
- HR questions: not reported in this account.

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Start early: Coforge tested the 2027 batch in pre-final year (April 2026) at CBIT.** Lyra should flag Coforge to 3rd-year students before April, not only to final-year students.
- **Practise a 90-minute aptitude test with no negative marking but sectional cut-offs.** Students should attempt every question and must not skip a whole section. The section names and question count are not published, so practise mixed timed sets.
- **Spoken English is a separate, scored test (SpeechX, 30 minutes).** Lyra can offer short speaking drills (read-aloud, listening, grammar) and remind students to test their mic and earphones before the test.
- **Interview:** the only candidate account (2024) focuses on SQL (DDL vs DML, simple queries, normalisation), OOP and basic data structures, plus simple coding such as Fibonacci. Prepare projects on the CV well.
- **Pay and marks cut-off are not published.** Lyra should not show any CTC or cut-off for Coforge as fact.

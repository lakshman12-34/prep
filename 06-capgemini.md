## 3. Selection steps

### Official (general fresher process) [S2]

1. Online aptitude test
2. Coding or technical assessment
3. Group discussion (for select roles only)
4. Technical interview
5. HR interview
6. Offer

- The FAQ describes this as the general process (see the S2 quote for its exact wording).
- Assessments are proctored online technical and language assessments, where applicable. Proctored = watched by camera or software during the test. [S2]

### Non-engineering drive (college notice, Feb 2026) [S4]

1. English assessment (listening, speaking, reading, writing)
2. Technical interviews

- Each round eliminates candidates. The drive was held online (virtual mode). [S4]

### What candidates report (3 accounts: Jan 2025, Jun 2025, Oct 2025)

- **Oct 2025, 2026 batch, on campus** [S7]: resume shortlisting → communication round, online from home (grammar and business-writing MCQs, then spoken English) → 4 days later, in college on the Cocubes platform: technical MCQs, compulsory coding, a game-based cognitive test, behavioural questions → one offline interview mixing technical and behavioural questions.
- **Jun 2025, on-campus diversity (female-only) drive** [S8]: technical + English + cognitive games + 75 psychometric questions → coding, only for top scorers → spoken communication test → technical interview.
- **Jan 2025, through Superset** [S9]: technical MCQs + English (including a 200-word paragraph) + games → coding, only for a higher-pay shortlist → communication round → interview on Superset.
- All 3 accounts say every round eliminates candidates. The place of the communication round differs between drives.

| Part | What candidates report | Source |

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **On campus:** Capgemini asks students to contact their college placement team. [S1]
- **Registration:** students register on the Superset portal, which Capgemini calls its recruitment partner, before applying. [S4]
- **When tests happened:** the 2026-batch on-campus test took place in Oct 2025 at one college (candidate account). [S7] Non-engineering drives ran in Feb 2026 [S4] and Apr 2026 [S5].
- **Off campus:** use Capgemini's India job search. [S3] On 3 Oct 2026 it listed 676 India jobs. Searches for exceller and fresher returned 0 results. So no off-campus Exceller opening was visible that day. [S3]
- **Fraud warning:** Capgemini says interview messages come only through its own hiring systems (ATS/CRM), with Microsoft Teams invites. Offers sent from personal email addresses should be reported to cg_interview_helpdesk.in@capgemini.com. [S2]
- **Dates for the 2027-batch season:** Not published.

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **The pattern is not published.** Capgemini does not publish its test pattern or fresher pay. Practise general aptitude and coding, and do not promise students a pay band.
- **Coding matters most.** In the one 2026-batch account, both coding questions were compulsory and the number solved decided the level (A4, A4(P) or A5). Practise solving 2 problems fully, not partly. The allowed languages are not published.
- **English, written and spoken:** all 3 accounts had grammar tasks and spoken English (read and repeat, listen and repeat, speak on a topic). Practise both.
- **Puzzle games and behavioural questions** appeared in the accounts. The names and number of games are not published, so practise general logic puzzles.
- **Interview:** the resume and projects came up in all 3 accounts. One 2026-batch candidate was asked on AI and its impact. For non-engineering students, note the 24x7 shift condition and the Aadhaar and DigiLocker requirement.

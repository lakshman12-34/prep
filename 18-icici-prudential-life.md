## 1. Tracks and pay

ICICI Prudential Life does not publish a page for each fresher programme. The names below come from its Annual Report FY2025-26. The report lists them as **induction programmes** for new recruits in FY2026 [S1, p.93 and p.125]:

| Programme (company's name) | Who it is for (company's words) | Onboarded in FY2026 | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **vSTAR** (the p.93 graphic shows "STAR") | "Sales frontline employees" | 7,000+ | [S1] |
| **Ascent** | "Operations employees" | 180+ | [S1] |
| **Excellerate** | "Technology & Data Sciences" | "~100" | [S1] |
| **Business Leadership Program (BLP)** | "Premier B-school hires (all)" | 300+ | [S1] |
| **MLDP Program** | "Premier B-school hires (Sales)" | 200+ | [S1] |
| New Manager Induction | "Sales managers (lateral hires)", so not freshers | 120+ | [S1] |

- The report does not say how many in each programme were freshers [S1].
- **vSTAR and Ascent** match the report's "entry-level sales and operations roles" for graduate freshers. This link is our reading; the report does not say it in one sentence [S1].
- **BLP and MLDP** are for premier B-school hires, so they are MBA routes, not routes for B.Com/BA/B.Sc graduates [S1].
- **Name clash:** the same report also describes a different "Business Leadership Program" for business heads and strategy leads (a leadership course for senior staff) [S1, pp.100–101]. Do not mix the two.

**Roles seen at campus drives** (college notices):

- Frontline Sales Executive, for M.Com final-semester students [S4] (undated).
- A virtual placement drive for 5th-semester B.A./B.Sc. students, on 30 Mar 2023 [S5]. This is old; we found no 2025–26 or 2026–27 notice.

**Pay:**

- CTC, stipend and incentives: Not published by the company.
- The careers site says: "Not just competitive fixed pay, but an opportunity for you to earn incentives with consistent performance." Benefits named: Mediclaim, group term and accident insurance [S2].
- One college notice: six students selected as Frontline Sales Executive "will be provided with an annual package of about Rs. 03 (three) lakhs along with other facilities" [S4]. It does not say whether this is CTC or in-hand, and it has no date.
- **Fee the student pays:** no fee is mentioned in any source we read. We found no paid training route like Axis Bank's.

## 2. Eligibility

- **Company report:** the 37% were "graduate/post graduate freshers" [S1]. The company also takes "fresh graduates from professional institutes and business schools" [S1, p.92].
- **Careers site:** "Candidates from all education backgrounds and professional experiences are welcome" [S2]. One employee story on the site is from a B.Com graduate who joined "as a trainee" [S2].
- **Campus Portal fields:** first name, date of birth, gender, email, mobile number, PAN number and college name. The portal says PAN, mobile and email "cannot be modified later" [S3]. The college list includes arts, commerce, science, BBA, BCA, management and engineering colleges [S3].
- **College notices:** M.Com final semester [S4]; 5th-semester B.A./B.Sc. (Hons and General) [S5].
- Marks cut-off, age limit, backlogs, gap rules: **Not published.**

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **This is a sales-first employer.** Most fresher hiring goes to frontline sales (vSTAR, 7,000+ onboarded in FY2026). Lyra should prepare B.Com, BA, B.Sc, BBA and BCA students for a sales interview: self-introduction, "why insurance", "why sales", handling rejection, and a short pitch of a life insurance plan.
- **The test pattern is not published.** The college notices only describe an interview. Practise general aptitude and English as a safety net, but put most effort into spoken communication.
- **Know the job before the interview.** The company's own page describes field travel, meeting customers, working with agents and monthly targets. Students should be ready to say they are comfortable with field work and targets.
- **Register correctly.** On the Campus Portal, PAN, mobile number and email cannot be changed later. Lyra can remind students to check these before they submit.
- **Pay is not published.** One college notice says "about Rs. 03 (three) lakhs" a year for Frontline Sales Executive. Lyra should show this as a single college report, not as the company's pay.

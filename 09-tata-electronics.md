> **How to read this file:** every fact has a source number like [S1]. The sources are listed at the bottom.
> **Official** = the company's own site, filings or results calls. **College notice** = a college placement notice on the college's own website. **News** = an established outlet quoting a named company official. **Candidate account** = a dated first-hand student account, used only for test and interview details.

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Test practice:** practise general aptitude and basic trade knowledge. The test pattern for the diploma, ITI and NATS routes is not published, so Lyra should say "pattern not published" on the card.
- **Show the route, not one pay figure.** NAPS stipends are ranges (₹6,800–17,000 and ₹8,200–13,500 a month), and job-fair pay is a band. Lyra should show the range and the year, never a single number.
- **Show eligibility clearly on the card:** TN portal job fairs are for women only; age limits change from listing to listing (18–23 up to 18–35); NATS posters say no arrears; diploma holders appear only on the Apr 2026 NATS poster.
- **Interview:** no candidate account was found. Lyra can offer a general HR mock interview, labelled as general practice, not a Tata Electronics pattern.
- **Safety message:** the company says it uses no third-party agencies and charges no fees. Lyra can show this as a warning on the card.

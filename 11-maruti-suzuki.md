## 2. Eligibility

### ITI Apprentice (official) [S2]

- **Age:** 18–23 years on the day of joining.
- **ITI council:** SCVT and NCVT only.
- **Institution:** Govt ITIs only (ITI pass-outs).
- **Passing year:** last 5 years up to the current year.
- **Trades:** Fitter, Welder, Painter (General), Electrician, Turner, Diesel Mechanic, Machinist, Tool & Die, Motor Mechanic Vehicle, Tractor Mechanic, and others as needed.

### ITI Flexi Workmen (official) [S3]

- **Age:** 18–26 years on the day of joining.
- **ITI council:** NCVT / SCVT only.
- **Trades:** same list as the apprentice route.

### Both ITI routes

- **Medical:** a pre-employment medical check-up [S1].
- **Marks cut-off:** **Not published.**
- **Gender rule:** **Not published.** Maruti says it has systematically increased the hiring of women in vehicle manufacturing and engine transmission roles at its Gurugram and Manesar plants (15 Apr 2026) [S6].

### Other routes

- **JET:** all eligibility claims are **Not verified** (see section 8).
- **GET 2026-27:** B.E./B.Tech [S11]. Other rules: **Not verified**.

## 2. Eligibility

Foxconn India publishes **no eligibility rules** for freshers. The only rules we could verify come from one older government-portal listing.

### 2023 Tamil Nadu portal listing (older, posted by a staffing contractor) [S6]

- **Qualification:** Diploma.
- **Gender:** female candidates only.
- **Age:** the job text says 18–30 only; the listing header says 19–32.
- **Experience:** 0–1 year.
- **Shifts:** rotating shifts: 6 am – 2 pm, 2 pm – 10 pm, 10 pm – 6 am.
- **Marks, branches, medical:** not stated.

### What officials have said about who works there

- **Devanahalli:** "nearly 80% women" (the minister's own words; M B Patil, Dec 2025) [S1].
- **Tamil Nadu:** Foxconn Chairman Young Liu said women are a big part of the Tamil Nadu workforce and that married women also contribute to Foxconn's growth (Aug 2024; older) [S4]. At the housing opening he said thousands of women from many regions of India would live there and work for Foxconn [S3].
- **Men:** no source says men are excluded from the plants. The only listing we found (2023) was for women only [S6].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Do not show a "Foxconn test pattern".** Foxconn India publishes no test, interview or selection steps, and we found no dated candidate account. Lyra should say: "Selection pattern not published."
- **The volume is real but the route is not a campus programme.** The verified numbers (30,000 jobs at Devanahalli; up to 50,000 expected in Phase 1) are shop-floor jobs, mostly women. Lyra can prepare diploma and ITI students, especially women, for shift work, hostel life and basic factory-floor questions, but should not promise a GET or DET route.
- **Teach students to check who is hiring.** The only government-portal listing we found was posted by a staffing contractor. A Lyra checklist could ask: Is the employer named? Is the listing on a government portal (tnprivatejobs, NCS) or a college notice? Is any fee asked?
- **Document checklist:** the 2023 listing asked for Aadhaar with a clear date of birth, certificates, bank account, PAN, photos and vaccination certificates. A ready-to-go document list is a simple, useful Lyra feature.
- **Tamil Nadu "14,000 engineering jobs" is not a hiring drive yet.** Foxconn said no new investments were discussed. Lyra should not list it as an open opportunity for B.E. students.

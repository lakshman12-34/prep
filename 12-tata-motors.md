## 2. Eligibility

### Lakshya 2026 (official) [S1]

- **Qualification:** final-year diploma, graduating in 2026, from a Government Polytechnic.
- **Branches:** Automobile, Electrical, Industrial Electronics, Mechanical, Mechatronics, Paint Technology.
- **Marks:** at least 60% from SSC onwards. No ATKT or backlog in any semester.
- **Background check and medical:** all selected candidates go through background verification and a medical check-up as per company standard.
- **Transfer:** selected candidates can be moved to any location, subsidiary or joint venture.
- **Age and gender:** **Not published.** The page says Tata Motors is an equal opportunity employer.

### Kaushalya 2026-27 (official) [S4]

- **Age:** 18 to 23 years.
- **Qualification:** 2-year ITI (NCVT / SCVT) or 12th pass (any stream).
- **Marks cut-off:** **Not published.**

### Trade Apprentice (official) [S6]

- **SSC / Class 10:** passed this year, in a single attempt.
- **Marks:** at least 60% in SSC (55% for SC/ST). At least 60% in Maths and Science for all categories.
- **Age:** 14 years and above, as per the Apprentices Act.

### Saksham 2026 (official; experienced hires) [S10]

- 2024 diploma, 60% throughout, 12–24 months of sales or service experience.

### Medical

- **Lakshya:** company-standard medical check-up [S1].
- **Other routes:** **Not published.**

## 3. Selection steps

**Lakshya 2026** (official) [S1]:
1. Shortlist: a merit list based on % of marks, from the top down. Only selected candidates are called for the test.
2. Online test.
3. Further selection process for shortlisted candidates (rounds not named).
4. Background verification and medical check-up before appointment.

**Kaushalya:** the posting says only shortlisted candidates will be contacted [S4]. The stages are **Not published.**

**Trade Apprentice:** online application form [S6]. Later stages: **Not published.**

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Lakshya test practice:** practise general aptitude and reasoning, basic maths, branch subject concepts and general English. The number of questions and the time are not published, so Lyra should say so on the card.
- **Marks decide who takes the test.** Lakshya shortlists on a merit list of % marks before the test, and needs 60% from SSC onwards with no ATKT or backlog. Lyra should show this clearly.
- **Show who can apply:** Lakshya is only for **Government polytechnic** students. Students at private polytechnics should not be shown Lakshya as open to them.
- **Show the company name correctly:** Lakshya is run by Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd; NAPS listings are under Tata Motors Ltd (commercial vehicles); Kaushalya appears under both.
- **Pay:** Lakshya, Kaushalya and Trade Apprentice pay are not published. Lyra can show the NAPS band (₹9,800–13,870 a month) and the government minimum stipend instead, each with its date.

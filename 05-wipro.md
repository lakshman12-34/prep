## 3. Selection steps

### Official (older pages)

| Track (page year) | Steps, in order | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Elite (2023) | Registration → Online assessment → Business Discussion → Letter of Intent and offer letter | [S2] |
| Turbo (2025) | Registration → Online assessment → Voice assessment → Business Discussion → Letter of Intent → Pre-skilling training → Offer letter | [S3] |
| STAR (2023) | Registration → Online assessment → Business Discussion → Offer | [S4] |
| WILP (2025) | Registration → 80-minute online assessment → 20-minute voice assessment → Business Discussion → Letter of Intent → Pre-skilling training → Offer | [S5] |

- Business Discussion is Wipro's name for the interview round after the online assessment. [S2]
- A Letter of Intent comes before the formal offer letter.

### What candidates report (2 accounts, Oct and Nov 2024)

- **10 Oct 2024**, on campus, Project Engineer [S13]: 4 rounds, each one eliminating: online test on AMCAT → communication round (listening, reading, spoken fluency) → technical interview (called the Business Round) → HR interview.
- **27 Nov 2024**, Elite Exclusive Hiring 2024 [S14]: online test → Business Discussion (technical) → HR discussion.
- In both accounts, the technical and HR parts were separate rounds.

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Official rules are only for older batches (2023 and 2025).** Treat 60% / 6.0 CGPA, 1 backlog and a 3-year gap as the last known rules. Students should check Wipro's Superset page when their own batch opens.
- **Test parts named on official pages:** aptitude (verbal, quantitative, logical), coding, and written communication. Practise a short timed essay. One 2024 account says the essay is marked on grammar, spelling and punctuation.
- **Coding:** the official Turbo and STAR pages list C, C++, Java and Python. Both 2024 candidate accounts had 2 coding questions. Practise solving 2 problems in one sitting.
- **Spoken English:** Turbo and WILP include a voice assessment, and one 2024 account had a communication round (listening, reading, spoken fluency). Students should practise speaking English aloud.
- **Interview:** both 2024 accounts asked on OOP and SQL. Students should also be ready for HR questions on relocation and the service agreement. FY27 has no fixed fresher target, so students should not rely on Wipro alone.

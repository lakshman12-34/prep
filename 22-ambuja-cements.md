## 1. Tracks and pay

### About the company (as of FY2025-26)

- Adani Cement (Ambuja Cements and its group) has **109 MTPA** capacity, **24 integrated units** and **22 grinding units**, with a presence in 31 states and union territories [S1].
- Sanghi Industries merged into Ambuja on 12 Mar 2026. Penna Cement merged into Ambuja on 10 Apr 2026 [S1].
- Ambuja's total workforce: **5,909** employees and workers in FY2025-26 (standalone) [S1].

## 2. Eligibility

### Apr 2025 campus drive at NRI Group of Institutions, Bhopal (Ambuja Cements, "Powered by Adani") [S4]

- **Qualifications:** Diploma (Civil, Mechanical, Chemical, E&I, Electrical, Mining); B.E./B.Tech (Mining, Mechanical); M.Sc. Geology; MBA Marketing.
- **Batch:** 2024 or 2025 pass-outs. 2024 pass-outs preferred.
- **Marks:** more than 55% in the highest qualification.
- **Backlogs:** no active backlogs.
- **Further study:** must not be studying further now.

### Jul 2026 GET (Civil), Technical and Sales roles (Government Engineering College, Ajmer notice) [S3]

- **Degree:** B.E./B.Tech in Civil Engineering.
- **Batch:** 2022 to 2026 pass-outs.
- **Marks:** at least 60% aggregate in 10th, 12th and engineering.
- **Other:** excellent communication skills; willing to travel within the assigned district; **valid driving licence**.

### Oct 2025 women's diversity drive (Infosys and Ambuja Cements) [S5]

- **Gender:** female candidates only.
- **Batch:** 2026.
- **Course:** B.Tech in any branch.
- **Marks:** at least 60% aggregate across academics.

### Other rules

- Age limit, medical rules, and any rule for ITI holders: **Not published.**
- Gender: no rule is published. Women hold "approximately 4%" of management positions (the company's own words) [S1]. 41 of the 1,548 new hires in FY2025-26 were women [S1].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Practise for interviews more than for a test.** The newest notice (Jul 2026, GET Civil) lists only a technical and an HR interview. The written test pattern is not published; the only accounts that mention an aptitude test are from 2019–2020. Lyra should say: "Practise general aptitude; the exact pattern is not published."
- **Core subjects matter.** One older account was asked about manufacturing, thermodynamics and industrial management; the other mentions "core technical questions". Lyra's branch-wise question banks fit this.
- **Put the eligibility rules on the card.** 55% in the highest qualification and no active backlogs (2025 drive); 60% in 10th, 12th and degree plus a driving licence for the 2026 Civil sales/technical role. A checker would stop students who cannot apply.
- **Diploma students: show the DET stipend clearly.** ₹2.10 LPA plus accommodation and meals (2025 drive). Tell students the 2026 DET figure is not yet published.
- **Volume is large, but notices come through colleges.** 1,500+ GETs and DETs joined in FY2025-26, yet no open posting was on the Adani portal on 3 Oct 2026. Lyra can remind TPOs to watch for Adani Cement drives and FutureX internship calls.

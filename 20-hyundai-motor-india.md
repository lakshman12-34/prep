**Words used in this file:** **Apprentice** = a trainee under the Apprentices Act who gets a monthly stipend, not a salary. **Designated trade** = an ITI-type trade fixed by the government (e.g. Fitter). **Optional trade** = a job-role course chosen by the company (e.g. Engine Assembly Technician). **BJT** = Basic Job Training (Hyundai's term). **OJT** = On-the-Job Training. **YTP** = Young Talent Pool. **NIRF** = the government's college ranking. **FY26** = April 2025 – March 2026.

## 2. Eligibility

### Apprentices (government apprenticeship portal, live 3 Oct 2026) [S3] [S4]

- **Gender:** Kanchipuram listings say **Male** (one Electrician listing gives no gender). Talegaon listings say **Male, Female**.
- **Minimum qualification shown on the portal:** 8th or 10th for the trade listings; Diploma for 3 listings (Chassis Marriage & Muffler Assembly, Engine Assembly, Body Painting & Sealer); ITI, 8th, 10th or 11th for some job-role listings. (These are the portal's course minimums. The listings do not add a marks rule.)
- **Age:** not shown on these listings.

- Passed out 2020, 2021, 2022 or 2023. Age limit 24 years. Gender: Male.
- Diploma branches: DME, DEEE, DPE, DAE, D.Robotics.
- ITI trades: MMV, Fitter, Turner, Welder, Mechanic Diesel, Machinist.
- This listing closed on 31 Jul 2024. No newer listing on that portal was found.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **Apprentices:** apply on **apprenticeshipindia.gov.in**. Listings are posted all year (Kanchipuram: Dec 2025 to Sep 2026; Talegaon: Aug to Oct 2026, two of them posted on 3 Oct 2026). Each listing has its own closing date, from Dec 2026 onwards [S3] [S4].
- **Engineer Trainee:** through campus engagement at YTP institutions [S1]. Dates: **Not published.**
- **Careers page:** hyundai.com/in has a careers page with no job list and no programme details (checked 3 Oct 2026) [S6].
- **Where you work:**
  - **Chennai plant (Plants 1 and 2):** Plot H-1, SIPCOT Industrial Park, Irrungattukottai, Sriperumbudur Taluk, Kancheepuram District [S1].
  - **Pune plant (Plant 3):** Plot A-16, MIDC Talegaon Industrial Area [S1]. Hyundai "operationalized its Talegaon manufacturing facility in Pune in 2025" [S8].
- **Fraud warning (official):** Hyundai "does not ask candidates to deposit or pay any money for employment". Report fake offers to the police, to 044-47100000 or to hiring@hmil.net [S6].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Main route for ITI and polytechnic students is the apprenticeship**, not a direct job. Lyra should send students to apprenticeshipindia.gov.in and show the live Hyundai listings (Kanchipuram and Talegaon), with the stipend range exactly as listed.
- **Show the gender rule clearly:** Kanchipuram listings are for men only; Talegaon listings are open to men and women.
- **Test practice:** Hyundai publishes no test pattern. Lyra should say "pattern not published" and offer general trade (technical) and aptitude practice only.
- **B.E. students:** the Engineer Trainee route runs through campus engagement at 20 NIRF top-75 institutions. Students from other colleges should know this route may not reach their campus; pay and test details are not published.
- **Safety message:** Hyundai never charges money for jobs. Lyra can show the hiring@hmil.net contact for reporting fake offers, and warn that the ₹23,400 / ₹19,400 figures seen on job sites are not confirmed.

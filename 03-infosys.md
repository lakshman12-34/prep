## 1. Tracks and pay

**Specialist Programmer (SP) and Digital Specialist Engineer (DSE)**

| Role | CTC |
|---|---|
| SP L3 (Trainee) | ₹21 LPA |
| SP L2 (Trainee) | ₹16 LPA |
| SP L1 (Trainee) | ₹10 LPA + ₹1 lakh joining bonus |
| DSE (Trainee) | ₹6.25 LPA + ₹75,000 joining bonus |

- LPA = lakh rupees per year.
- **Where these figures come from:** college notices. Three of them reproduce Infosys's own mail. The same figures appear for:
  - the class of 2025, off-campus (notice dated 24 Dec 2025) [S10];
  - the batch of 2026, campus drive (notice dated 5 Jan 2026) [S12];
  - the class of 2026, off-campus (notice dated Apr 2026) [S11];
  - the 2027 campus programme (notice dated 7 Jul 2026) [S13].
- **Systems Engineer (SE):** pay Not verified. We found no Infosys page and no college notice for the 2026 or 2027 batch that gives SE pay. Only job blogs and prep sites give a figure.
- **Power Programmer:** Infosys has a Power Programmers page, but it gives no fresher pay and no fresher hiring route [S8]. Not verified as a current fresher track.
- **HackWithInfy route:** Infosys says HackWithInfy finalists may get pre-placement interview chances for the Specialist Programmer role and other roles in its specialised units [S4]. Which result leads to which role: Not published.

## 3. Selection steps

- **SP/DSE, class of 2026 off-campus** [S11]:
  1. Round 1: virtual (online) assessment.
  2. Round 2: in-person assessment, followed by an in-person interview.
  - A separate HR round: Not published.
- **SP/DSE, class of 2025 off-campus:** in-person assessment at named test venues across India [S10].
- **HackWithInfy** [S5]:
  1. Round 1.
  2. Round 2, for those who qualify in Round 1.
  3. The top 100 from Round 2 go to the 4-day Grand Finale.
- **SE:** Not published by Infosys.

**What candidates report, SE** (2 candidate accounts, Nov–Dec 2025) [S14] [S15]:

- An online or in-person test, then one interview that mixes technical and HR questions.

## 4. Online test

**SP / DSE** (college notices):

- Round 1 and Round 2 are each 3 hours long, with programming tasks of different difficulty levels [S11].
- Class of 2025: 3 hours, a programming-based test with questions of different difficulty levels [S10]. The notice attaches sample questions labelled EASY, MEDIUM, HARD and COMPLEX [S10].
- Number of questions, coding languages, cut-offs, and how SP vs DSE is decided: Not published.

What candidates report (1 second-hand account, Jan 2026; written by a friend of the candidate): 3 coding questions, medium to hard [S16].

**HackWithInfy** (official) [S5]:

- Round 1: a 3-hour coding test.
- Round 2: a 3-hour coding test for those who qualified in Round 1.
- The coding tests are hosted on the Infosys Assessment Platform (IAP) [S5].
- Infosys publishes sample questions [S6].
- Round 1 was virtual (March 2026); Round 2 was face-to-face (April 2026) [S4].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **SP/DSE coding:** each round is a 3-hour programming test, with questions from easy to complex. Students should practise long, timed coding sessions. The number of questions is not published; one second-hand account reports 3.
- **Official practice material:** Infosys publishes HackWithInfy sample questions [S6]. HackWithInfy rounds are also 3-hour coding tests, so Lyra can use this set as a model for SP/DSE-level practice.
- **SE test:** Infosys does not publish the SE pattern. One Nov 2025 account lists logical, technical, verbal, pseudo-code, puzzles, grammar and a short essay in 120 minutes, and another reports a cut-off in each section. Practise general aptitude, pseudo-code and short essay writing; the exact pattern is not published.
- **Interview:** SE candidates describe short interviews built on the project, "Why Infosys?" and strengths and weaknesses. The mock interview should drill a clear project explanation and these HR basics.
- **Registration:** the SP/DSE notices say college email addresses cannot be processed. Lyra can remind students to register with a personal email.

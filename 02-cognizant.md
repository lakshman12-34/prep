## 1. Tracks and pay

- **Role title:** Cognizant calls the GenC Pro role "Programmer Analyst (PA)" [S2]. Other role titles: Not published.

**Pay.** Cognizant's own pages give no pay for GenC or GenC Next [S1]–[S3]. The figures below come from a college notice and one Cognizant page on an older drive.

In one account (Oct 2025), 22 people were selected: 8 for GenC Next and the rest for GenC [S21]. Cognizant does not describe it on the pages we checked.

## 2. Eligibility

**Engineering (GenC, GenC Pro, GenC Next)**

- **2026 batch** [S2]: specific B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech streams. Streams such as Leather, Food and Fashion Technology are excluded. Programming knowledge is required. MCA and M.Sc are not listed.
- **2027 batch** [S3]: B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech students graduating in 2027. The same streams are excluded, and dual-degree students are also excluded.
- **Marks:** Not published on the 2026 and 2027 Cognizant pages. For the **2025-batch** off-campus drive: at least 60% in Class 10, Class 12, diploma, UG and PG; 59.9% is not rounded up [S10].
- **Backlogs:** for the 2025 batch, students with standing arrears (uncleared backlogs) were not eligible [S10]. 2026/2027 rule: Not published.
- **Other 2025-batch rules** [S10]:
  - Branches for that off-campus drive: CS, IT, AI&DS, EC.
  - You must accept any Cognizant office location, shift timing and technology.
  - Open to Indian nationals and OCIs living in India.
- **Education gap:** Not published for 2026/2027. An **old** Cognizant page for the **2022 batch** said students with standing arrears or a gap of more than 2 years in education were not eligible [S6].
- **Age:** Not published.
- **Ace Team, 2027** [S4]: students graduating in 2027 with B.E./B.Tech/M.E./M.Tech degrees. At CBIT, the ACE interviews were for B.E./B.Tech CSE, IT and AI&DS students [S15].

**Analyst Trainee (3-year degrees)**

- 2025 graduates, or students graduating in 2026, with a three-year full-time degree (BCA, BSc, BA, BCom, BBA, BVoc, BMS and similar). At least 50% in academic records [S5].
- 49.87% or 49.9% is not accepted [S12].
- Students now in a full-time postgraduate course are not eligible [S12].
- You join as a fresher. Earlier work experience is not counted [S5].

## 3. Selection steps

**Official steps, GenC** [S1]:

1. Apply through a campus drive or an open position.
2. Initial screening.
3. Assessments of communication, aptitude and/or technical skills.
4. Interview, online or in person.
5. Results go to your placement officer (campus) or to you (off-campus).

- Cognizant says not every candidate goes through every step, and the process may vary [S1].
- **2026 on-campus:** every role needs a communication test, aptitude and technical tests, and a technical/SME interview [S2].
- **Analyst Trainee:** you get only one interview attempt. Interviews are online [S12].

**What candidates report** (6 candidate accounts, Jun 2025–Sep 2026):

- **GenC route:** Communication → Aptitude (with games) → Technical test → one combined Technical + HR interview [S19] (Jun 2025). Another account had a written test first, then communication, then a Technical + HR interview [S17] (Dec 2025).
- **Digital Nurture route** (3 accounts agree [S18] [S21] [S22]): Communication → coding test → skill training (8 weeks in one account [S21]; the other gives the length in days [S18]) → MCQ test → interview.
- **How the track is decided:** Not published. In one account the GenC Next / GenC split came after the interview [S21].

## 4. Online test

**Official facts:** Cognizant names three tests: communication, aptitude and technical [S1] [S2]. It does not publish the number of sections, questions, timings, coding languages, negative marking, adaptive rules or cut-offs.

**What candidates report** (6 candidate accounts, Jun 2025–Sep 2026):

| Item | What candidates report | How many accounts |
|---|---|---|
| Communication test | 60 minutes [S19]. Listening, reading and speaking [S17]. Reading, listening, grammar, and speaking on a topic [S18]. An audio clip played only once that you must repeat [S19]. | 3 agree that speaking and listening are tested |
| Aptitude test | 30 aptitude questions, then 4 sections of game-based tasks; 80 minutes [S19] | 1 (Jun 2025) |
| Technical test | 2 coding + 2 SQL + 1 web (HTML/CSS/JS) question [S18] [S19] [S20]. A fourth account reports 2 SQL + 2 Java questions [S22]. | 3 agree on 2 + 2 + 1 (Jun 2025, Oct 2025, Sep 2026) |
| Technical test time | 120 minutes [S20] | 1 (Oct 2025) |
| Languages | Options were Java, Python and C# [S19]. A C# cluster [S20]. A Java cluster [S18] [S21] [S22]. | 5 |
| Platform | Applications are on Superset. One interview was recorded on Superset [S21]. | 1 |
| Negative marking, adaptive questions | Not reported | 0 |
| Cut-offs | Not published | 0 |

"Cluster" means the technology group you are tested in (for example Java or C#).

## 5. Interview topics

Candidate accounts only: 6 accounts, Jun 2025–Sep 2026 [S17]–[S22].

- **Projects and resume:** a deep look at your projects [S17] [S18] [S19] [S21].
- **Coding:** in your cluster language, with simple array and string problems reported [S17]–[S22]. One candidate was told to write the code in Java only [S19].
- **OOP and Java basics** [S17] [S18] [S19].
- **SQL:** SQL vs PL/SQL [S21]; Nth highest salary, TRUNCATE vs DROP, joins [S22].
- **Digital Nurture:** the interview also covers the stack taught in training, for example a React counter task [S18].
- **HR:** willingness to relocate and to work flexible hours, including night shifts [S17] [S19] [S21].
- **Length:** 40 minutes [S18] (the candidate's own estimate; see the source list) and 55–60 minutes [S21].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Spoken English:** Cognizant says every 2026 role has a communication test. Three candidate accounts say it tests listening and speaking (for example, repeating an audio clip and speaking on a topic). Students should practise these, not only reading and grammar.
- **Coding plus SQL:** three candidate accounts report a technical test of 2 coding questions, 2 SQL questions and 1 web (HTML/CSS/JS) task. Students should practise SQL queries next to coding, in Java, Python or C#.
- **Aptitude:** the exact pattern is not published. One account reports 30 questions plus 4 game-based sections. Practise general aptitude and short puzzle-type games.
- **Interview:** prepare for one combined technical and HR round: projects, OOP, SQL basics (joins, Nth highest salary, TRUNCATE vs DROP) and questions on relocation and night shifts.
- **Eligibility:** Cognizant has not published the 2026/2027 marks rule. The 2025-batch rule was 60% everywhere with no standing arrears. Lyra can use 60% and no backlogs as a safe target, and say clearly that it is the older rule.

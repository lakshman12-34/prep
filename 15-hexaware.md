## 1. Tracks and pay

Hexaware's financial year is the calendar year (CY) [S1].

**Programmes Hexaware names** [S1] [S2]:

- **Mavericks:** the graduate talent programme. Hexaware says it turns "engineering and non-engineering graduates" into skilled employees, with onboarding, project exposure, mentoring and skill modules for each track [S2]. In 2025, new Mavericks went through the "Blended Learning Foundation program" [S1].
- **HFLX (Hexaware Future Leaders and Executives):** a fast-track management trainee programme for graduates of leading business schools [S2].

## 2. Eligibility

**2025, 2026 and 2027 engineering batches:** Not published. We found no Hexaware page or college notice with the rules.

**Junior Management Trainee, 2026 batch** [S3]: passing batch 2026. Other rules: not stated in the notice.

**GET, 2021 batch** (official Hexaware PDF; old, shown for reference only) [S8]:

- BE/B.Tech/MCA, passing year 2021.
- Branches: CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, ETC, EIE, ICE and allied branches.
- 60% or 6.0 CGPA throughout Class 10, Class 12 and UG/PG (till the last announced results).
- No active backlogs.
- Education gap: not more than 1 year. A gap within the course is not allowed.
- Work locations: Chennai, Bangalore, Mumbai, Pune, Noida.

## 3. Selection steps

**Official steps:** Not published.

**What candidates report** (2 candidate accounts: published 9 Oct 2024 and 10 Jan 2025; both 2025 batch, on campus) [S6] [S7]:

1. **Online test** (aptitude and domain).
2. **Group discussion (GD)**, in person at the college. Groups of 20 [S7]; 15–16 [S6].
3. **HR interview.** One account had it online on the Superset platform, 10–20 minutes [S6].
4. GET offer. One account got a letter of intent the next day [S7].
5. **PGET only:** students eligible for PGET take a **coding assessment**, then an **EC (effective communication) interview**. If they do not clear the coding round, they are offered GET [S6] [S7].

**Funnel in one college** (1 account, Aug–Dec 2024) [S6]: 430 eligible → 242 cleared the online test → 75 cleared GD → 43 GET offers. For PGET: 24 took the coding test → 13 cleared → 7 PGET offers.

## 5. Interview topics

Candidate accounts only: 2 accounts, Oct 2024 and Jan 2025 [S6] [S7].

- **GD topics:** "Impact of AI in employments", deadlines and productivity, hard work vs smart work, city life vs village life [S6]; a topic on psychological counselling [S7]. One HR let the group choose its own topic [S6].
- **HR:** introduce yourself; willingness to relocate; what you know about the company; where you see yourself in 5 years [S6]; what you would change about your college; handling failure; handling many tasks at once [S7]. **Both accounts were asked about the 2-year service agreement** [S6] [S7].
- **EC interview (PGET):** about 25 minutes in one account; self-introduction, final-year project, questions on your own coding-test answers, family, projects [S6].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Hexaware does not publish its current engineering-trainee pattern or pay.** Lyra should say so and point students to their college notice. The 2021 GET rules (60% throughout, no active backlogs, gap of at most 1 year) are the last official ones; use them only as a warning sign, not as the rule.
- **Online test:** practise quant, reasoning and English, plus **pseudocode reading** and computer fundamentals. Both candidate accounts mention pseudocode, which many other companies do not test.
- **Group discussion matters.** Both accounts had an in-person GD with large groups. Lyra's mock interview can add GD practice on current topics (AI and jobs, hard work vs smart work).
- **For PGET:** practise 2 easy-to-medium array problems in 40 minutes in C, C++, Java or Python, and be ready to explain your own code in the EC interview.
- **HR:** students should understand the 2-year service agreement before the interview; both accounts were asked about it.

## 3. Selection steps

Official order, from the FY26 ASE posting [S1]:

1. Mock (practice) assessment, 20 minutes. It is compulsory.
2. Assessment 1: Behavioural and Cognitive.
3. Assessment 2: Technical.
4. Assessment 3: Coding.
5. Assessment 4: Communication. It comes with a separate email and login.
6. Final round: interview.

- All 4 candidate accounts from the 2025-26 season (Dec 2025 – Jul 2026) describe the interview as one combined technical + HR round [S11]–[S14].
- The older Packaged App posting (FY24 event) used a different order: cognitive and technical together, then coding, then communication [S2].

## 4. Online test

**Short answer on the "new 2026 pattern":** Accenture's own FY26 ASE posting confirms the sections and their times [S1]. It gives a question count only for coding (3 questions). It gives no cut-offs. The earlier version's total of "125 questions, 175 minutes" is Not verified.

### Official pattern (FY26 ASE posting)

Source: [S1]. The FY26 ASE posting for Persons with Disabilities has the same text [S2].

| Section | What Accenture lists | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mock | Practice test, compulsory | Not published | 20 min |
| Behavioural + Cognitive | Behavioural: personality. Cognitive: gamified puzzles and numerical equations | Not published | 40 min for both parts together |
| Technical | Common applications and MS Office; pseudocode; basics of networking, security and cloud; basic programming concepts | Not published | 45 min |
| Coding | Backend: Java, Dotnet, Python, C++, C#. Database: SQL. Front end: HTML, CSS, React.js, Vue.js | 3 | 60 min |
| Communication | Verbal: pronunciation, fluency, active listening, understanding spoken English, vocabulary, articulation, grammar. Written: etiquette, content, grammar | Not published | "approximately 30 minutes" (Accenture's words) |

- **Total questions and total time:** Not published. Accenture gives only the section times above.
- **Pseudocode** = program steps written in plain words instead of a real programming language.

**Other official facts:**
- **AI tools:** external AI tools are not allowed. Some coding questions may include a built-in AI assistant [S5]. This FAQ covers all Accenture India roles.
- **Pass marks:** the FY26 posting does not say which sections eliminate. The older Packaged App posting said candidates must meet the section and subsection cut-off [S2]. The general careers FAQ says candidates who reach the set threshold move to the next stage [S5]. The cut-off number is Not published.
- **Adaptive test** (questions change with your answers): Not published.
- **Negative marking:** Not published.
- **Test platform:** Not published for the campus test. The MyZone portal's own files are served by HirePro [S1] [S3]. The general careers FAQ suggests HackerRank's free community to get used to the coding screen. That FAQ covers all roles, not only campus [S5].

### What candidates report

4 on-campus candidate accounts from the 2025-26 season: Dec 2025 [S11], Jan 2026 [S12], Apr 2026 [S13], Jul 2026 [S14].

- **Coding:** all 4 accounts describe 3 tasks: an SQL query, a backend/DSA problem, and a front-end HTML/CSS/JavaScript task [S11]–[S14]. (DSA = data structures and algorithms.)
  - The Jan 2026 account rates SQL medium, backend slightly tough and front-end easy [S12].
  - The Jul 2026 account says the backend task at that campus allowed only Java and Python [S14].
- **Games (cognitive part):** Dec 2025: maze path-finding, pattern recognition and small calculations [S11]. Jul 2026: memory-based games [S14].
- **Behavioural:** the Dec 2025 account reports 50 situational/behavioural questions [S11].
- **Technical MCQ topics:**
  - Dec 2025: networks, cloud, pseudocode [S11].
  - Apr 2026: computer architecture, operating systems, networking, DevOps, bitwise operations, MS Office/Excel [S13].
- **Communication:** the Apr 2026 account describes a voice, listening and grammar test, plus email writing [S13]. The Dec 2025 candidate took it from home [S11].
- **Which rounds eliminate: the accounts disagree.**

| Round | Accounts saying it does not eliminate | Accounts saying it must be cleared |
|---|---|---|
| Games | Jan 2026 [S12], Jul 2026 [S14] | Apr 2026 [S13] |
| Communication | Jan 2026 [S12], Apr 2026 [S13] | Jul 2026 [S14] |

- **How the track is decided:** the Jan 2026 account says the communication score played a part in the final role and package [S12]. The Jul 2026 account says that at their campus, only candidates who solved all 3 coding questions were shortlisted [S14].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Practise the official sections and times:** behavioural + puzzle-style cognitive (40 min), technical MCQs on MS Office, pseudocode, networking, security, cloud and basic programming (45 min), and 3 coding tasks (60 min). Question counts are not published, so Lyra should not show them as fact.
- **Coding practice should cover three kinds of task:** an SQL query, a backend problem (Java, Dotnet, Python, C++ or C#), and a front-end HTML/CSS/JavaScript task. The official language list and 4 candidate accounts point to this mix.
- **Communication has a spoken and a written part.** Students should practise both speaking and short business writing such as emails.
- **Interview:** students should be ready to explain their own project and why they chose its tech stack, and to answer basic HR questions (why Accenture, family, a conflict, a failure).
- **Eligibility checks in Lyra:** use the official rules (no active backlogs, no gap in the current degree, full-time study, 3-month gap since any earlier Accenture process). Do not show a marks cut-off or pass mark; Accenture does not publish one.

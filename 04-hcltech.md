## 3. Selection steps

### AMP (official) [S1] [S2]

1. Cognitive test
2. Coding/Technical test
3. Versant English test. Only for those who clear steps 1 and 2.
4. Technical interview
5. HR interview
6. Top 10% only: more interviews, hackathons or technical tests, for Momentum or Polaris.

A news report on Primus campuses gives the same order: cognitive, coding/technical and English tests, then interviews. [S13]

### TechBee (official) [S9]

1. Registration
2. HCL CAT (a career aptitude test: quantitative, logical reasoning, language)
3. Versant (includes spoken English)
4. Face-to-face interview

### What candidates report (2 accounts: Jan 2025 and an Oct 2025 drive)

- **Oct 2025 drive, 2026 batch** (Parul University) [S14]: online MCQ test → offline English proficiency test (24 Oct 2025) → online technical interview → HR interview. Four stages, partly online and partly on campus.
- **14 Jan 2025, 2025 batch** [S16]: this drive also had a group discussion (topic: pros and cons of AI) and three interviews. This is one drive only.

## 4. Online test

### Official (AMP) [S1] [S2]

| Part | What it covers | Time or pass mark |
|---|---|---|
| Cognitive test | Numerical, verbal, reasoning | "~90 minutes" (the source's own wording) |
| Coding/Technical test | Coding or technical questions | 60 to 90 minutes |
| Versant test | English speaking and listening | Pass score 46–56 (B1 level) |

- Only candidates who clear the Cognitive and Coding tests take Versant. [S1] [S2]
- Questions per section: Not published.
- Coding languages: Not published.
- Negative marking: Not published.
- Adaptive or not: Not published.
- Test platform: Not published.
- Cut-offs: only the Versant score (46–56) is published. [S1] [S2]

### Official (TechBee HCL CAT)

- Sections: Quantitative Reasoning (Mathematics), Logical Reasoning, English language. [S10]
- No fee to take the test. [S11]

### What candidates report (2 accounts: Jan 2025 and an Oct 2025 drive)

- **15 Jan 2025, 2025 batch** [S15]: 75 questions in 90 minutes. A mix of coding exercises, technical MCQs (DBMS, OOPS, CN, ST, OS) and logical, verbal and quant questions.
- **Oct 2025 drive, 2026 batch** [S14]: an online MCQ test, then a separate offline English proficiency test.

MCQ = multiple-choice question. DBMS = database management systems. OOPS = object-oriented programming. CN = computer networks. OS = operating systems. The account does not expand ST.

## 5. Interview topics

From 3 candidate accounts: 14 Jan 2025 and 15 Jan 2025 (both 2025 batch), and an Oct 2025 drive (2026 batch).

| Account | Technical round | HR round |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 2025 drive, 2026 batch [S14] | Operating systems, DBMS concepts, OOP principles, applied problem-solving in DSA (data structures and algorithms). Detailed questions on her projects. | Behavioural questions, role-fit questions, joining details (start date, location) |
| 15 Jan 2025, 2025 batch [S15] | Projects and internships on the resume. The five pillars of OOP. Normalization in DBMS. Favourite language, with its pros and cons. | Mostly situation-based and managerial questions |
| 14 Jan 2025, 2025 batch [S16] | Skill-based questions (Power BI scenarios). Then a separate Tech HR round on resume skills and projects. | An HR round (topics not given) |

- All 3 accounts include projects or the resume.
- 2 of the 3 accounts include OOP and DBMS.

## 6. Season and how to apply

- **Campus season:** HCLTech has completed its FY27 fresher hiring. It visited engineering campuses from July. The news does not say which year. [S12] [S13]
- **Primus campuses:** more than 60 partner engineering colleges get hiring priority. [S12] The official page says Primus colleges have an industry-aligned curriculum. [S1]
- **Off campus:** the Apply now button on the campus hiring page goes to freshers.hcltech.com. [S1] On 3 Oct 2026 the portal said it was under a brief pause, and showed no openings. [S3]
- **Campus Naukri Young Turks 26:** the campus hiring page promotes it as a skill-based assessment, with a link to naukri.com. [S1] Whether it leads to HCLTech offers: Not published.
- **Other campus programmes on the official page:** Campus Ambassador Program; AMPlified: The AI Hackathon (token-based scoring); Campus Talks; Aikyam, a forum for TPOs (training and placement officers). [S1]
- **TechBee:** apply on hcltechbee.com. Test dates come by email. [S7] This FAQ is out of date: it still says registrations are open for 2023 Class 12 pass-outs. [S7]
- **Dates for the next season:** Not published.

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Marks gate first.** AMP needs 70% in Class 10, Class 12 and graduation (65% at New Vistas locations). Flag students below this early.
- **Aptitude:** practise numerical, verbal and reasoning questions for a cognitive test the official page lists as "~90 minutes". The number of questions per section is not published.
- **Coding/technical:** the test runs 60 to 90 minutes. HCLTech does not publish the languages or the question count, so practise general coding in the student's main language.
- **Spoken English:** the Versant test comes before the interviews, and the pass score is 46–56 (B1). A student who does not clear it does not reach the interviews.
- **Interview:** in all 3 candidate accounts (2025 and 2026 batches), projects or the resume came up. OOP and DBMS came up in 2 of the 3. Students should practise explaining their own projects in detail.

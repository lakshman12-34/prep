## 1. Tracks and pay

TCS has three tracks (TCS calls them cadres): **Prime, Digital and Ninja**. Your NQT score decides which interview you are called for [S1].

**Pay (CTC) for the Feb–Mar 2026 drive** [S1] [S7]. This is the official page for that drive. We found no newer drive page on tcs.com (3 Oct 2026).

| Track | UG, 0–1 yr experience | PG, 0–1 yr experience | UG, 1–2 yr experience | PG, 1–2 yr experience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Prime** | ₹9.09–9.30 LPA | ₹11.59–11.80 LPA | ₹9.45–9.66 LPA | ₹12.05–12.26 LPA |
| **Digital** | ₹7.09–7.30 LPA | ₹7.39–7.60 LPA | ₹7.50–7.72 LPA | ₹7.82–8.04 LPA |
| **Ninja** | ₹3.4 LPA minimum | ₹3.6 LPA minimum | Not published | Not published |

- LPA = lakh rupees per year.
- TCS says the actual pay depends on relevant work experience and job location [S1].
- Internship experience is not counted when TCS fixes your salary [S1].
- This drive focused on Prime and Digital. Ninja offers depend on performance in the hiring process [S1].
- **Other TCS entry routes** listed on TCS's entry-level page (read 3 Oct 2026): BPS Hiring 2027, Atlas (M.Sc/MA Economics), MBA Hiring 2026, BSc Ignite 2026, Sigma (pharmacy) and CodeVita Season 13 [S2]. We did not check their pay or rules.

## 2. Eligibility

All rules below are from the official drive page for the Feb–Mar 2026 drive [S1]. The RGUKT college notice repeats the same text [S7].

- **Batches:** 2024, 2025 and 2026.
- **Degrees:** B.Tech/B.E., M.Tech/M.E., MCA, M.Sc/M.S in any specialisation, from AICTE/UGC-recognised universities or colleges.
- **Marks:** at least 60% aggregate, or equal CGPA, in Class 10, Class 12, diploma (if any) and graduation. All subjects are counted.
- **CGPA to percentage:** percentage = 10 × CGPA.
- **First class:** the offer is valid only if the degree or provisional degree certificate clearly shows First Class.
- **Backlogs:** no pending backlog when you take the TCS selection process.
- **Course time:** no extended education. You must finish the course in its normal duration.
- **Education gap:** at most 24 months in total, up to your highest qualification. Every gap must be declared.
- **Course type:** full-time courses only. Part-time and correspondence courses are not accepted.
- **Open schooling:** NIOS Class 10 or Class 12 is accepted if your UG/PG was full-time.
- **Work experience:** up to 2 years.
- **Age:** 18 to 28 years. The date on which age is counted: Not published.
- **Existing offer:** if you already hold a TCS offer, you cannot apply for TCS off-campus hiring.

## 3. Selection steps

**Official steps** [S1]:

1. Register on TCS NextStep (IT category) and click **Apply For Drive**.
2. Take the TCS NQT in person at a TCS iON centre.
3. Based on your test score, you are shortlisted for a Prime, Digital or Ninja interview.
4. Interview. TCS does not list the interview rounds. It says you are judged mainly on the skill you chose at registration, along with the normal interview.
5. You fill the application form after the test results. Original documents are checked at the interview and at joining.

**What candidates report** (4 candidate accounts, Feb–Jun 2026) [S9]–[S12]:

- All 4 describe three interview parts: **Technical, Managerial and HR**, on the same day, in person.
- In 2 of the 4 (May and Jun 2026), all three interviewers sat in one panel [S11] [S12].
- Time from test to result: 6 days [S11], one week [S12], 33 days [S9].

## 4. Online test

- TCS calls it one integrated 190-minute test with two mandatory sections [S1].

- Questions per section: Not published.

## 5. Interview topics

Candidate accounts only: 4 accounts, Feb–Jun 2026 [S9]–[S12].

- **Technical:**
  - Projects: asked in 3 of 4 accounts [S9] [S10] [S12]. In the fourth, the panel did not focus much on the resume [S11].
  - The questions came from the skill chosen at registration (Core Java) [S11].
  - Language basics: how Python and Java run, lists vs arrays [S10]; multithreading, deadlock, HashMap vs HashTable [S11].
  - SQL: a JOIN with UPDATE [S10]; SQL data types [S12].
  - Writing short code on paper [S11] [S12].
  - Cloud, when it is on the resume: AWS EC2, Lambda, S3 [S9] [S12].
  - Operating-systems questions were not reported in any of the 4 accounts.
- **Prime (1 account):** backend code for the project's modules, and a DSA problem, "House Robber" (LeetCode 198) [S9].
- **Digital (1 account):** questions on a machine-learning project [S10].
- **Managerial:** SDLC, Agile, Waterfall vs Agile, handling team conflicts [S9]; a scenario on learning a new skill in one week [S11]. One managerial round asked the name of TCS's current CEO [S9].
- **HR:** willingness to relocate and comfort with night shifts [S10]; a shift question [S11] [S12]. The accounts also mention a document check [S9]–[S12].

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Aptitude:** practise Numerical, Verbal and Reasoning sets of 25 minutes each, the official section times. TCS does not publish the number of questions, so practise timed sets, not a fixed question count.
- **Advanced part:** practise harder quant and reasoning (25 minutes) and coding (90 minutes). This part is required for Digital and Prime. Three candidate accounts report 2 coding questions; two of them describe one easier and one harder question.
- **Coding language:** candidates report C, C++, Java, Python and Perl as options. Practise in one of these. TCS does not publish the official list.
- **Interview:** prepare for three parts in one sitting. Technical: projects, language basics, SQL and short code on paper. Managerial: SDLC, Agile and team scenarios. HR: relocation and night shifts. Questions follow the skill chosen at registration, so students should choose a skill they know well.
- **Eligibility check:** Lyra can warn students before they register if they are below 60% anywhere, have a pending backlog, have a gap over 24 months, or lack a First Class degree. Under TCS's published rules, these students are not eligible.

## 1. Tracks and pay

Tech Mahindra's annual report lists "Campus Technical (fresh engineering/technology graduates)" as one of its hiring categories. It also lists Management Trainees (recent B-school graduates) and BPO hiring [S4].

**Pay for the 2024 batch** (newest official figures we found; from a Tech Mahindra email copied on the RGUKT website, registration 16–19 Jul 2024) [S6]:

| Track | Pay | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Regular** (pass the regular selection process) | ₹3.25 L a year CTC | 6 months' probation |
| **Supercoder** (pass the extra Supercoder test) | ₹5.5 L a year CTC | 6 months' probation |
| Both | – | Indemnity bond of ₹1 L for a mandatory 2-year service period from joining |

- We found no pay figure for the 2025, 2026 or 2027 batch from Tech Mahindra or a college notice (3 Oct 2026).
- During probation, the trainee may get unit-specific classroom training, on-the-job training or shadowing [S6].

**Other entry routes:**

- **TechM CODE (2027 batch):** a national hackathon. The prize for top performers is a direct pre-placement offer (PPO) for the **Expert Programmer** role at **₹18 LPA CTC, including joining bonus**. Cash prizes worth ₹6 L. Open to pre-final-year students (2027 batch) in CS, Mathematics, EE, or with a DS minor. Registration closed 3 May 2026 [S7].
- **Customer Support Associate (BPS):** a voice role for any graduate, 2025 and 2026 batches, **₹2.2 LPA**, rotational shifts including night shifts. The college says this "is not their regular B Tech/MCA/MBA hiring" [S8].
- **BPS pooled drive:** in Nov 2024, Tech Mahindra's Business Process Services division ran a pooled drive for the 2025 batch at Vidya Academy; 16 CSE and ECE students were selected [S12]. Pay: Not published.

## 2. Eligibility

**2024 batch** (registration 16–19 Jul 2024; official email copied on the RGUKT website) [S6]. This is the newest full set of rules we found. Treat it as old.

- **Courses:** BE/B.Tech in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, IT, E&C, E&TC, Telecom, Communications, Cyber Security or Electronics; or MCA; or M.Sc (Computer Science, Information Technology, Cyber Security).
- **Marks:** at least **70% or 7 CGPA**, without rounding up, in Class 10, Class 12/diploma and the degree. Tech Mahindra says rounding off marks to 70% is "NOT valid".
- **Batch:** finished the course in 2024.
- **Backlogs:** none. You must have a consolidated marksheet or provisional degree certificate.
- **Gap:** no gap of more than one year after Class 10 and before the graduate/postgraduate course. No loss of an academic year after joining a course.
- **Work:** open to working anywhere in India, at TechM centres or client sites, and in different shifts.
- **2025, 2026 and 2027 batches:** Not published. We found no official page or college notice with the rules.
- **Age limit:** Not published.

## 3. Selection steps

**Official steps, 2024 batch** [S6]:

1. Only eligible candidates get the online test link, by email. Each test is a filter for the next stage.
2. **Aptitude Test + English Essay/Story** (online).
3. **Technical & Psychometric Test** (online).
4. **Verbal Communication Test** (online).
5. **Technical Interview** at a TechM campus.
6. **HR Interview** at a TechM campus.
7. **Supercoder:** candidates with strong academics who do well in the regular rounds (HR round not counted) can take the Supercoder Test. Those who pass are "Supercoders".

**What candidates report** (2 candidate accounts: published Jul 2025 and Jan 2026) [S9] [S10]:

- Both describe online tests first, then a technical interview and an HR interview.
- Jan 2026 (on-campus): aptitude, essay and coding were on the same day, and each round was eliminatory. The interview call came "after approximately 15 days". Interviews were in person at a Tech Mahindra office [S9].
- Jul 2025 (Associate Software Engineer): aptitude + essay + coding, then an AI-proctored spoken "vocabulary test" taken at home, then a 30-minute technical interview and a 15-minute HR interview [S10].
- Note: the Jul 2025 account names a former CEO. The current CEO is Mohit Joshi [S2]. So the process in that account may be older than its publish date.

## 4. Online test

**Official facts (2024 batch)** [S6]:

- Three online stages: (1) Aptitude Test and English Essay/Story; (2) Technical & Psychometric Test; (3) Verbal Communication Test.
- Number of questions, minutes per section, coding questions, languages, negative marking, platform and cut-offs: all **Not published**.

**What candidates report** (2 accounts, Jul 2025 and Jan 2026):

- **Aptitude:** quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning and verbal ability (both accounts) [S9] [S10].
- **Essay:** one account says candidates were shown an image and wrote 150–200 words about it [S10]. The other says topics were general or technology-related [S9].
- **Coding:** **2 questions** in both accounts [S9] [S10]. Examples: second smallest element in an array; check if two strings are anagrams [S10].
- **Languages:** C, C++, Java, Python (1 account) [S10].
- **Spoken test:** an AI-proctored round: repeat sentences, fill in blanks with the right word, describe images in one sentence. Background noise could disqualify you (1 account) [S10].
- **Psychometric test, question counts, timings, negative marking, platform:** not reported in these 2 accounts.

## Our notes for Lyra

*This section is our analysis, not company data.*

- **Treat the pattern as unknown for 2026-27.** The newest official rules are for the 2024 batch. Tell students to check their college notice for the current marks cut-off and pay. In 2024 the cut-off was a strict 70% / 7 CGPA with no rounding up, so Lyra should not round marks when it checks eligibility.
- **Practise the published stages:** aptitude (quant, reasoning, verbal), a short timed essay, and a technical/psychometric test. TechM does not publish question counts or times, so practise timed mixed sets, not a fixed count.
- **Coding:** both candidate accounts report 2 questions on basics (arrays, strings). Practise easy-to-medium array and string problems in C, C++, Java or Python.
- **Spoken English:** both the official 2024 notice (Verbal Communication Test) and one candidate account (AI-proctored speaking round) point to a spoken test. Practise repeating sentences, short image descriptions and basic grammar, in a quiet room with a headset.
- **Interview:** prepare projects, OOP with code examples, SQL joins, DELETE vs TRUNCATE, network basics, SDLC/Agile, and a clear "yes" on relocation and shifts. Strong coders in the 2027 batch should watch for TechM CODE (₹18 LPA PPO in 2026).
